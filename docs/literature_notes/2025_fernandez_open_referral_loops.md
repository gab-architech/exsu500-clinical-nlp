# Perspectives on Delayed Specialty Follow-Up After a Primary Care Visit

- **Citation:** Fernandez L, Ricci D, Pollack A, et al. Patient Perspectives on Delayed Specialty Follow-Up After a Primary Care Visit. J Am Board Fam Med. 2025;38(1):139-153. doi:10.3122/jabfm.2024.240063R3
- **Read by:** Jessica, 28 October 2027
- **Peer-reviewed?** yes (Q1 journal)

**What they did:** Qualitative interviews (n = 30) with patients whose dermatology or cardiac stress-test referrals were not completed on time, from 305 "open-loop" patients found by EHR review at two US primary care clinics.

**Main result:** Hundreds of time-sensitive referrals (suspicious skin lesions, chest pain) were left open; patients described communication and system breakdowns contributing to delays.

**Limitation:** Qualitative, small sample, mostly one clinic, during COVID-19. 

**Relevance to our project:** Real-world evidence that referrals fall through the cracks, motivating better routing of clinical documents.
